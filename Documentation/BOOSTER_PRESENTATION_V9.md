# Sculpted accelerator presentation, v0.9

Art direction: [generated booster concept](ArtDirection/V9/BoosterConcept.png). The native Unity model follows its pearl-alloy drive plates, dark machined cassette, cyan recessed lighting and curved side fins. It uses original procedural geometry rather than a screenshot applied to a rectangular card.

The old broad flat pad is replaced by a visible recessed dark cassette with three separate thick, dark-beveled chevron plinths and inset pearl faces. Narrow luminous inlays, curved side fins, dark lamp recesses, bright endcaps and small flush fasteners complete the model. Both opaque mesh groups cast shadows; the alloy receives lighting and reflections. Cyan acceleration, amber launch and jade conveyor lights have distinct shader flow rates. An activation pulse and a 14mm cosmetic lift react without moving the trigger or altering ball physics.

Every pad still uses three renderers. A course still owns seven shared reward/pad meshes and ten shared materials; no per-pad material clones or colliders are introduced. The complete accelerator mesh recipe is below 1,800 triangles. Assembly elevation is recessed 0.11m because authored trigger centers sit around 0.10-0.13m above the road; the central drive faces stay around 6-9cm above that roadway. A minimum cosmetic width of 1.15m keeps the fins outside the rolling sphere on narrow pads; the authored trigger size remains unchanged.

BoosterArtV9Tests covers geometric depth, separate white plates over continuous dark recesses, finite normals/triangle budget, shared resources, variant state, activation/reset isolation, native material closeups and an actual force-driven boost trigger crossing. Its test runway is an explicitly authored capture fixture using the shipping LevelBuilder/GameSession, independent of rebuilt campaign coordinates. Closeups are 1280 x 960 model-inspection views; the runway and activation captures use the portrait gameplay camera. The first integrated slice captured real boost activation, but art review found the bright surfaces too flat. After the dark-cassette/edge-contrast refinement, all ten selected booster/presentation/native/menu tests passed in 10.60 seconds: [BoosterPolish-v9.xml](../TestResults/BoosterPolish-v9.xml). Native review confirmed the darker base, clear directional plates, readable cyan/amber/jade variants and rendered menu silhouettes. These are original stylized mobile models; they do not reproduce every detail of the generated concept image.
