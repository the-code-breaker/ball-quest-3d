## Exact prompt: level concepts

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: level design concept sheet for an original native Unity mobile rolling-ball game, Ball Quest 3D. Create one beautifully finished landscape concept sheet with SIX distinct playable elevated obstacle-course concepts arranged in a clean 3 by 2 grid. Each panel combines a readable three-quarter isometric complete route and a small near-ball chase view. All routes must be physically coherent wood/ceramic/metal tracks for a 1m diameter ball; show safe landing platforms and challenging rail-free narrow sections. Six layouts: 1) a tight stacked canyon hairpin descent with dangerous 1.8m wide balance beams; 2) a tall clockwise corkscrew with a bridge crossing over its earlier track; 3) an asymmetric figure-eight overpass above a blue harbor; 4) a chain of floating volcanic islands with a boost runway leading to a controlled drop and a generous landing; 5) a clockwork gauntlet with swinging hammers and staggered timed bridges; 6) an icy zigzag needle-beam staircase with alternating banks and braking pockets. Premium stylized 3D mobile-game rendering: rounded industrial rails, warm planked deck, dimensional gold coins, dark glossy ball with small cyan accents, sculpted rock/tree islands, airy atmospheric depth, clear contact shadows, restrained cinematic glow. Original designs inspired by the visual readability of casual rolling-ball games. Every panel must have a drastically different route footprint and height profile, not repeated S-shaped tracks. Show difficult traversal, not broad flat highways. Avoid UI, logos, watermarks, copied characters, photographic realism and illegible decoration. Tiny labels only: HAIRPIN ABYSS, CORKSCREW CITADEL, INFINITY CROSSING, VOLCANIC DROP, CLOCKWORK TRIAL, FROST NEEDLES. This is art direction concept artwork, not a claim of an actual screenshot.

## Exact prompt: booster

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 3D game prop design sheet for an original Unity rolling-ball game. Show a premium accelerator runway booster as a polished native 3D prop, not a UI icon. Landscape industrial design sheet: one large close three-quarter hero view on the left, two orthographic top/side views on the right, and a small chase-camera gameplay context at the bottom. The prop sits flush within a warm maple elevated track, has three separated raised forward-pointing arrow plates, soft bevels, pearl ceramic lips, dark graphite recesses, brushed brass fasteners, short sculpted side fins and cyan light strips tucked into deep channels. Runway length 5 metres, width 3 metres, player sphere diameter 1 metre; surface rise no more than 0.12 metres so it looks driveable. Arrows and the light channels provide strong forward motion; a restrained rising luminous pulse and a few fine speed streaks indicate activation. Glossy black ball with cyan insets nearby for scale. Clearly show geometry depth and attractive material contrast, never a flat rectangular blue doormat or oversized luminous billboard. Add tiny complementary launch variant in warm amber and conveyor variant in emerald. Premium playful mobile 3D art, clean silhouettes, soft studio key light, ambient occlusion, tasteful bloom, clear physically plausible assembly, neutral blue backdrop. No brand marks, no logo, no copied Going Balls art, no advertising text, no UI. This is concept art to guide a real-time mesh implementation.
