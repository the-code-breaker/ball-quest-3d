Focused runs overlap and are not added together as unique tests. Route counts are content coverage inside integration tests, not separate Unity test cases. Current versioned raw XML/logs are retained. The current evidence verifier combines the 34-case EditMode run, separate pilot and campaign cases, 96-case retained-regression run and latest five-case tactile/native rerun by unique test name. All 132 cases pass; the rerun adds no unique cases. The full Android package verifier also passed.

The route comparison resamples 32 points after removing translation and uniform scale. Its closest pair differs by 0.105357 normalized RMS units, above the 0.08 duplicate threshold. This catches copied or uniformly resized footprints. The narrowest road is 1.65 metres around a one-metre ball. Structural tests cover safe entries/checkpoints, drop catches and separated remote crossings. A difficulty flag is not a measurement of human difficulty.

The traversal driver issues the same physical force/brake commands as normal control. It never teleports the ball, writes pose/velocity, disables obstacles or changes gravity to pass. All 55 routes crossed actual ordered checkpoints and finish triggers. Physical simulation times ranged from 34.9 to 146.55 seconds; course construction ranged from 30.42 to 124.09 ms, median 51.97 ms in the Editor. The serialized physics/core-controller baseline is retained in [campaign-physics-baseline-v9.json](../TestResults/campaign-physics-baseline-v9.json). It does not certify arbitrary later source edits; visual/audio changes require separate review.

The ball-only gesture test observed 180 actual Input System event frames, maximum side/forward speeds of 2.111/1.692 m/s, zero camera input commands and maximum viewport error about 0.000000566. The camera's composition target remains x=0.50/y=0.40. Internal orbit APIs survive for diagnostics and fixtures; normal human touch and mouse drags have no camera mapping.

Contact source analysis found a maximum narrow-band tone-energy fraction of 0.00432 in 25-350 Hz and maximum tested absolute autocorrelation of 0.03571. In the contact test, rolling pitch changed from 0.9813 to 1.0095 across the tested low/high speeds. Six ripple slots were bounded to at most three active during that sequence and physical impacts still animated with audio muted. Final native review enlarged the visible ripple from an initial 0.65-metre diameter to a 1.15-2.15-metre diameter, depending on impulse, over its unchanged 0.24-second lifetime; [the contact capture](../TestResults/Previews/V9ContactFeedback.png) now shows the ring beyond the ball. These are signal/lifecycle checks, not a human preference or phone-speaker audition. The [42-second dry audition](../TestResults/Audio/ContactFoleyV9-Audition.wav) is available for listening.

Generated [concept images](ArtDirection/V9/README.md) are separate art-direction references. Native gameplay uses original Unity geometry/materials and does not reproduce every illustrated detail. Quarter-route automated captures can show an empty HUD progress bar because the human progress follower ignores external test control; physical traversal is recorded independently. Debug overview/inspection poses are not physical completion evidence.

The current eight-ball Editor sample used an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 Ti and 240 target-paced frames. Mean/median/p95 frame intervals were 16.6653/16.6779/16.9313 ms. It recorded 299 enabled course renderers, 276 collider components, a 56.74 ms build and 205.34 MiB Unity allocation. One generation-zero collection occurred and maximum whole-frame managed allocation was 551,664 bytes. Draw-call, SetPass and triangle counters were unavailable and recorded as -1. Separately, 600 warmed isolated reward-animation calls allocated zero managed bytes on their calling thread. Neither measurement establishes zero-allocation gameplay, GPU cost or sustained phone FPS.

The native-view fixture captures debug overviews and physically supported inspection poses for IDs 1-11 and 55. Examples include [the coil overview](../TestResults/Previews/V9Family03Overview.png), [the figure-eight overview](../TestResults/Previews/V9Family05Overview.png), [the island descent](../TestResults/Previews/V9Family06Overview.png), and [the final precision beam](../TestResults/Previews/V9Family55Precision.png). These show actual Unity geometry/finishes and use explicitly selected inspection poses; separate traversal reports establish completion.

The 96-case aggregate retained regression, final ripple refinement and Android build/package checks passed; the serialized physical baseline remains unchanged after building. Package checks verified signature, manifest/version, ZIP CRC, 16 KiB ZIP/native alignment, both scenes, all adventure/race definitions, the three new deck maps, all 30 referenced Foley clips and excluded test-only code. The certificate matches v0.8, so an update is expected to preserve application data, but physical installation/update was not tested. The final ADB check lists no phone. Hardware-specific flicker, touch latency/comfort, perceived difficulty, speaker/headphone quality and sustained thermal/FPS behavior remain unverified on a physical device. Current Editor/package evidence does not establish those phone results.
